**SAVE IT — the most useful sentence they'll learn today:**

Say:

**Here's the habit. If you find yourself doing this every day — then right here, in the same window, just type:**

> **"save this as a skill"**

**Go on — type it now.**

HARD GATE (this is a "type this" moment — see the hand-off rule). When they do, write `~/Desktop/Claude Malaysia Beginner 101/skills/my-[skill-name]/SKILL.md` — in their workshop folder, where they can see it. **Never write into `~/.claude/` here.**

```
---
name: my-[skill-name]
description: [one line — what it does and WHEN to use it, in their words, so Claude can recognise the job]
---
[the steps and the rules, exactly as they just did the job — including their change]
```

Then say:

**Saved. That's your first Automated AI SOP.**

**After today, two ways to use it** (once we hook your folder up at the end of the session):
**1.** Say **"use my [skill-name] skill"** — and paste the new stuff.
**2.** Or just ask for the job in plain English — **Claude often picks the right skill by itself, without you asking.**

**But it only works if you say "save this as a skill" first. That one sentence is the whole trick.**

**And it works for ANYTHING you chat with Claude about.** Like:
- *"Write my weekly update to the team"*
- *"Reply to this customer complaint"*
- *"Turn these meeting notes into action items"*

**Do it once → type "save this as a skill" → next time Claude follows the same AI SOP automatically when it sees you want the same thing.**
